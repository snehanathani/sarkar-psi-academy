## 🌟 Overview & Architecture

This repository contains **3 bespoke, high-performance, aesthetic dark-mode website templates** designed specifically for **Sarkar PSI Academy** to showcase their coaching excellence in Maharashtra Police Sub-Inspector (PSI), MPSC Rajyaseva, Combine Group B & C, and competitive government examinations.

The root of this project serves as an **Interactive Client Review Hub** allowing the client to test drive all three design directions across Desktop, Tablet, and Mobile viewports with live WhatsApp lead routing.

## 🎨 3 Distinct Aesthetic Design Directions

### 1. Template 1: `v1-cadet-dark` — Tactical Cadet (Navy & Police Gold)
- **Design Philosophy:** Authoritative, disciplined, insignia-driven aesthetic inspired by defense and police academies.
- **Palette:** Tactical Midnight (`#060911`), Navy Surface (`#0B1120`), Police Khaki Gold (`#D4AF37`, `#F5C842`).
- **Signature Features:**
  - **Interactive PSI Physical Eligibility Calculator:** Candidates can input gender, height (cm), and chest (cm) to instantly check their physical qualification for Maharashtra PSI.
  - **Dadar (Mumbai) Expansion Spotlight:** Prominent highlighting of Prabhadevi Mandir Dadar batches (Wed, Thu, Fri - 2 hrs daily) & Mira Road Thane center.
  - **Hall of Fame & Telemetry:** 7,500+ successful appointments, 25+ years track record.
  - **Author's Book Catalog:** Highlighting the bestselling "PSI Thokla" and "Rajyaseva Pre Thokla".

### 2. Template 2: `v2-prestige-editorial` — Prestige Editorial (Obsidian & Emerald Bronze)
- **Design Philosophy:** Scholarly, dignified civil services monograph styling with editorial serif typography.
- **Palette:** Obsidian Void (`#070A0E`), Slate Charcoal (`#0E131B`), Imperial Emerald (`#10B981`), Antique Champagne (`#E6D5AC`).
- **Signature Features:**
  - **7-Stage Pedagogical Framework:** Systematic walkthrough from foundational syllabus mapping to mock interviews.
  - **The Author's Desk:** Comprehensive literary showcase of Prof. Ravindra Sarkar's published books with leather-bound visual cards.
  - **Curriculum Ledger Table:** Detailed breakdown of MPSC Rajyaseva (Class 1 & 2), Combine Group B/C (STI, ASO), and Saral Seva.
  - **Heritage Monograph Feel:** High intellectual credibility suited for Class 1 & 2 aspirants.

### 3. Template 3: `v3-modern-bento` — Metro Bento Edition (Cyber Slate & Electric Amber)
- **Design Philosophy:** High-conversion modern Bento-grid design inspired by cutting-edge digital platforms (Apple / Linear / NextLeap).
- **Palette:** Carbon Void (`#090C12`), Cyber Slate (`#121928`), Electric Amber (`#FF7800`), Neon Glows.
- **Signature Features:**
  - **Modular Bento Grid Hero:** Dynamic cards for Dadar Batch, 7,500+ Selections Counter, Prof. Sarkar Mentorship, and Books.
  - **Interactive Filterable Course Matrix:** Dynamic tabs ("All", "PSI & Police", "MPSC Rajyaseva", "Combine B/C", "Saral Seva") switching cards in real time without page reload.
  - **Quick Admission Fast-Docket:** Streamlined inquiry builder with instant WhatsApp direct messaging.
